## Home & energy

- **[Tibber](https://tibber.com/se)** — hourly spot-price electricity in Sweden. I use it with Home Assistant to shift big loads (EV charging especially) to the cheapest hours instead of paying peak price on autopilot. They just launched a referral bonus: sign up through my link and we both get 1,000 kr in the Tibber Store, or 6 months with no Tibber fee.
